About the Platform

The Fire Hazard & Patient Tracking System (FHPTS) is a comprehensive digital platform designed for emergency response coordination, fire hazard management, and patient tracking across Bangladesh.

Public Access: Anyone can report fire hazards and emergencies

Inter-Agency Coordination: Fire Service, Hospitals, and Authorities

24/7 Operations: Round-the-clock emergency response

Real-time Tracking: Monitor incidents and patient status instantly

Portal Access Roles

System Administrator

Full system control, hospital registration, authority management

Hospital Authority

Patient management, staff coordination, emergency records

Fire Service Officer

Hazard monitoring, response coordination, incident management

Medical Staff

Patient updates, admission records, treatment tracking
